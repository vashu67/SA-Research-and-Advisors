Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between financial complexity and strategic execution for startups, SMEs, and growth-stage businesses. We achieve this by:

  • Driving Predictability via FP&A: Delivering structured budgeting, 13-week cash flow modeling, and dynamic financial projections that transform historical data into forward-looking strategic clarity.
  • Unlocking True Worth through Valuation Support: Providing robust, investor-ready DCF models, comparable company analysis, and transaction multiples to give founders a definitive understanding of their market value.
  • Enabling Data-Backed Decisions: Equipped with monthly MIS dashboards and precise variance analysis, we ensure management teams and investors maintain absolute visibility over profitability and financial performance.
Vision

To be the premier FP&A and valuation advisory partner for high-growth enterprises, empowering leadership teams with the financial foresight and institutional-grade clarity needed to scale confidently and maximize shareholder value.

Our Services

Comprehensive financial advisory solutions covering every pillar of the modern finance function.

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)

We transform your historical financial data into a forward-looking strategic roadmap, ensuring your leadership team maintains absolute control over liquidity and growth.

Budgeting & Planning
Structured annual and quarterly budgets designed to align directly with your operational planning and business growth objectives.
Cash Flow Forecasting
Implementation of 13-week cash flow models, proactive working capital planning, liquidity forecasting, and burn rate analysis to secure runway.
Fundraising Financial Models
Dynamic, investor-ready financial models including detailed projections, scenario/what-if analysis, unit economics, runway analysis, and valuation sensitivity.
Monthly MIS Reporting & Dashboards
Monthly MIS dashboards, KPI tracking, budget vs. actual variance analysis, and comprehensive investor reporting packs to keep stakeholders aligned.
Working Capital Management
Optimization of receivables, payables, inventory, and cash conversion cycles to enhance liquidity, improve operational efficiency, and maintain healthy cash flow.
CapEx Planning
Strategic capital expenditure planning, investment evaluation, ROI assessment, and multi-year budgeting to optimize resource allocation and support sustainable growth.
Business Valuation Support & Strategic Advisory

Whether you are preparing for a funding round, an M&A transaction, or internal restructuring, we provide independent, institutional-grade valuation mechanics.

Valuation Methodologies
Rigorous execution of Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation, comparable company analysis (CCA), and precedent transaction multiples.
Early-Stage Frameworks
Tailored startup valuation approaches designed specifically for pre-revenue or early-growth companies where traditional metrics may not apply.
Strategic Advisory
Deep-dive analysis to help founders understand the core drivers of their corporate value and how to optimize them before approaching investors.
Research & Investor Communication
Industry research, white papers, custom financial reports, market intelligence, sector benchmarking, investor-ready brochures, and pitch deck preparation.
