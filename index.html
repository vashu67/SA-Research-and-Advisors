Strategic Finance Advisory

SA Research and Advisors is a strategic finance advisory firm specializing exclusively in high-impact Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) and Business Valuation Support. We act as a trusted extension of your leadership team to bridge the gap between complex financial data and strategic execution.

Our Services

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)

We transform your historical financial data into a forward-looking strategic roadmap, ensuring your leadership team maintains absolute control over liquidity and growth.

  • Budgeting & Planning: Structured annual and quarterly budgets aligned with operational planning and business growth objectives.
  • Cash Flow Forecasting: 13-week cash flow models, liquidity forecasting, and runway analysis.
  • Fundraising Financial Models: Investor-ready financial models with projections and scenario analysis.
  • MIS Reporting & Dashboards: KPI tracking, variance analysis, and investor reporting packs.

Business Valuation Support

Whether preparing for fundraising, M&A, or strategic planning, we deliver structured valuation support with analytical depth and investor alignment.

  • DCF Valuation Models: Detailed discounted cash flow models with assumptions and sensitivity analysis.
  • Startup & Growth Valuation: Valuation support tailored for high-growth companies and fundraising rounds.
  • Investor Presentation Support: Financial insights and valuation narratives for investor discussions.
