Our Services

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)

We transform your historical financial data into a forward-looking strategic roadmap, ensuring your leadership team maintains absolute control over liquidity and growth.

  • Budgeting & Planning: Structured annual and quarterly budgets designed to align with growth objectives.
  • Cash Flow Forecasting: Implementation of 13-week models and liquidity planning to secure runway.
  • Fundraising Financial Models: Investor-ready models including scenario analysis and valuation sensitivity.
  • MIS Reporting & Dashboards: Monthly KPI tracking and budget vs. actual variance analysis.

Business Valuation Support

Whether preparing for funding, M&A, or restructuring, we provide independent, institutional-grade valuation mechanics.

  • Valuation Methodologies: Rigorous execution of DCF, comparable company analysis, and precedent transactions.
  • Early-Stage Frameworks: Tailored approaches for pre-revenue or early-growth companies.
  • Strategic Advisory: Analysis to help founders understand and optimize core value drivers.
