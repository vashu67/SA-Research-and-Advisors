Strategic Finance Advisory

SA Research and advisors is a strategic finance advisory firm specializing exclusively in high-impact Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) and Business Valuation Support. We act as a trusted extension of your leadership team to bridge the gap between complex financial data and strategic execution.

Our Services

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)

We transform your historical financial data into a forward-looking strategic roadmap, ensuring your leadership team maintains absolute control over liquidity and growth.

  • Budgeting & Planning: Structured annual and quarterly budgets designed to align directly with your operational planning and business growth objectives.
  • Cash Flow Forecasting: Implementation of 13-week cash flow models, proactive working capital planning, liquidity forecasting, and burn rate analysis to secure runway.
  • Fundraising Financial Models: Dynamic, investor-ready financial models that include detailed projections, scenario/what-if analysis, unit economics, runway analysis, and valuation sensitivity.
  • MIS Reporting & Dashboards: Monthly MIS dashboards, key performance indicator (KPI) tracking, budget vs. actual variance analysis, and comprehensive investor reporting packs to keep stakeholders aligned.

Business Valuation Support

Whether you are preparing for a funding round, an M&A transaction, or internal restructuring, we provide independent, institutional-grade valuation mechanics.

  • Valuation Methodologies: Rigorous execution of Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation, comparable company analysis (CCA), and precedent transaction multiples.
  • Early-Stage Frameworks: Tailored startup valuation approaches designed specifically for pre-revenue or early-growth companies where traditional metrics may not apply.
  • Strategic Advisory: Deep-dive analysis to help founders understand the core drivers of their corporate value and how to optimize them before approaching investors.
